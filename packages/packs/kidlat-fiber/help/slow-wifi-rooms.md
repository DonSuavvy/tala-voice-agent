title: Slow Wi-Fi in some rooms
tags: slow, wifi, room, rooms, weak, signal, mabagal, mahina, kwarto
---
If the internet is fast near the modem but slow in other rooms, the Wi-Fi signal is not reaching those rooms well.

Put the modem in an open, central spot, up high, such as on a shelf. Keep it away from thick walls, metal, mirrors, aquariums and microwave ovens.

Do not keep the modem inside a cabinet or behind the television.

Walls and floors weaken Wi-Fi. For a room far from the modem, a device plugged into the modem with a cable is faster than Wi-Fi.

If the internet is slow even right next to the modem, the problem is not the Wi-Fi. Tell us on the hotline, and we can check your connection.
