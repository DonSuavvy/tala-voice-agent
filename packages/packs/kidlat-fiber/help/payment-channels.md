tags: pay, payment, bill, bayad, magbayad, gcash, maya, bank, 7-eleven

You can pay your bill through GCash, Maya, your bank's app or online banking, at any 7-Eleven store, or at a Bayad payment center.

Have your account number ready: you need it to pay through any of these.

Keep the reference number the payment channel gives you. If a payment does not show on your account, that number is how we find it.
