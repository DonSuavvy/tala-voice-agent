title: Restarting your modem yourself
tags: restart, reboot, modem, turn off, off and on, i-restart, patayin, buksan
---
Restarting the modem clears many small problems, such as a slow or dropped connection.

Press the power button on the modem to turn it off, or unplug its power adapter. Wait about ten seconds, then turn it back on.

Give the modem a few minutes to start up. The Power light comes on first, and the PON light turns steady once the modem is connected again.

Do not press the small reset hole with a pin. That erases your Wi-Fi name, your password and the modem's other settings.
