title: Changing your Wi-Fi name or password
tags: wifi, password, name, network, ssid, palitan, pangalan
---
You can change your Wi-Fi name and password yourself, from a phone or computer connected to the modem.

The sticker on the back or bottom of the modem shows the address of its settings page, and the login for it.

Open that address in a web browser and log in with the details on the sticker.

Find the Wi-Fi or WLAN settings. Type the new name or the new password, then save.

Your devices will disconnect. Connect them again with the new name and password.

A strong password is long and hard to guess. Do not share it with people outside your home.
